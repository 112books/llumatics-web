## What is Caffenol?

Caffenol is a homemade photographic developer made from everyday ingredients: instant coffee, sodium carbonate and vitamin C. It was first formulated in 1995 at the Rochester Institute of Technology and has since become the go-to reference for experimental and eco-friendly film developers.

The main developing agents are **caffeic acid** and **chlorogenic acid** found in coffee, which donate electrons to the silver ions in the emulsion and reduce them to metallic silver. Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) reinforces and stabilises the action of the coffee, while sodium carbonate (washing soda) acts as the accelerator: it raises the pH of the solution and activates the reaction.

The result is a developer that genuinely works, is environmentally friendly, can be prepared at home and costs very little.

## Ingredients for 1 litre of Caffenol

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Distilled or filtered water (Brita) | 1,000 ml |
| Sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃), anhydrous | 54 g |
| Ascorbic acid (pure vitamin C powder) | 16 g |
| Instant coffee (100% coffee, not decaffeinated) | 40 g |

> **Important**: sodium carbonate is NOT caustic soda (sodium hydroxide, NaOH). They are two very different products. Sodium carbonate is safe with basic precautions; caustic soda is corrosive and dangerous. Always read the label carefully.

## Preparation step by step

### 1. Dissolve the sodium carbonate
Dissolve the 54 g of sodium carbonate in 500 ml of water. It must dissolve completely with no lumps. A hand blender (stick blender) works very well for achieving a homogeneous solution.

### 2. Prepare the coffee solution
Dissolve the 40 g of instant coffee in the remaining 500 ml of water. Add the 16 g of ascorbic acid and stir until dissolved. Leave to rest for **5 minutes** — during this time the coffee and vitamin C integrate and the mixture changes colour slightly.

### 3. Combine and rest
Mix the two solutions together and leave the combined mixture to rest for a further **10 minutes** before use. The Caffenol should be at **20 °C** when you use it.

## Development process

### Pre-soak
Fill the developing tank with **tap water** (unheated) and insert the reel. Leave for **1 minute** to clean the emulsion and soften the film. Empty the tank.

### Developing with Caffenol

Pour the Caffenol into the tank and start timing:

- **First minute**: agitate continuously
- **Remaining minutes**: agitate for the **first 10 seconds** of each minute, then rest for the remainder

**Indicative development times:**

| Film speed | Time |
|---|---|
| ISO 100 | 20 minutes |
| ISO 400 | 25 minutes |

These times are a starting point. Variables (coffee brand, actual carbonate concentration, exact temperature) can affect the result. Experiment and keep notes.

### Stop bath (water rinse)
Instead of a chemical stop bath, do **4 changes of water at 20 °C** (approximately 1 minute in total). The water changes remove coffee stains from the emulsion and allow the fixer to be preserved for future reuse.

### Fixing
Fix the negative with a standard black-and-white fixer dissolved in **distilled water**. Fixing time: **5 minutes** with moderate agitation.

### Final rinse
Finish with a bath of **distilled water** with **1 drop of wetting agent** per litre (Kodak Photo-Flo, Ilford Ilfotol or equivalent). The wetting agent prevents drying marks and promotes even drying.

## Characteristics of Caffenol

Caffenol produces very distinctive results, quite unlike an industrial developer:

- **Visible grain**: Caffenol is not a fine-grain developer. Grain is part of the character of coffee-developed images.
- **Medium contrast**: it works particularly well with moderately contrasty films. Avoid very harshly lit scenes.
- **Warm tones**: the negative may show a slight brownish cast, especially if the development time is exceeded.
- **Single use**: Caffenol is discarded after each development. It is not a replenishable developer.
- **Smell**: the process produces a strong coffee smell. Please ensure the working space is well ventilated.

## Recommended films

| Film | Exposed ISO | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ilford HP5 Plus | 400 | The classic for Caffenol. Very consistent results. |
| Kodak Tri-X 400 | 400 | Pronounced grain and character. A great pairing. |
| Fomapan 400 | 400 | Affordable, excellent for experimentation. |
| Ilford FP4 Plus | 100 | Fine grain, very clean negative. Good choice for 20 min. |
| Kodak T-Max 100 | 100 | Very fine grain; may need timing adjustments. |

Avoid chromogenic black-and-white films (Kodak BW400CN, Ilford XP2): they are designed for C-41 processing and do not give good results with Caffenol.

## Safety and the environment

- Wear **nitrile gloves** when handling sodium carbonate and ascorbic acid powder.
- Work in a **ventilated** space: the coffee smell is strong and persistent.
- Caffenol is a very low-toxicity developer. Artisanal volumes (1 L) can be poured down the drain with plenty of running water, given their low environmental impact.
- Label your solutions as you prepare them (carbonate / coffee+vitamin C / Caffenol ready) to avoid mix-ups.
