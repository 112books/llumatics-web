Street photography is not learned in a weekend workshop. It is learned on the street, with time, with mistakes and with a lot of patience. But you learn it far better if you have a frame: references to argue with, a group to share the process with, and someone who reads your work and asks you uncomfortable questions.

*[From Street to Book](/en/tallers/del-carrer-al-llibre/)* is Llumàtics' annual course in analogue street photography. Twelve monthly sessions, each devoted to a photographer or a foundational movement of the genre. Joan Colom and Català-Roca to start at home. Cartier-Bresson, Robert Frank, Winogrand, Diane Arbus, Saul Leiter, Koudelka, Moriyama, Alex Webb and Martin Parr to widen the gaze. Every session is a different universe. Every session asks the same question: *how would you do it?*

The format is simple: study of the author in the morning, a practical outing around Barcelona in the afternoon, individual assignments between sessions. One roll per session. Maximum six people. At the end of the year, a real body of work and the possibility of turning it into a photobook.

**This is not a course for everyone.** It is for those who already know how to shoot in manual and want to go deeper into street photography as a conscious practice, with criteria and with the will to build a style of their own. Anyone who signs up knows they will need better shoes than a better camera.

The first edition starts in **October 2026**. Six places only.
