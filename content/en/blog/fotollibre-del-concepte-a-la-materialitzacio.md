Having images is the starting point. Turning them into a book is a completely different decision. A photobook forces you to choose — which images go in, which do not, in what order, in whose voice — and every decision defines what the whole thing is about.

The new **[Photobook: from concept to object](/en/tallers/fotollibre/)** workshop is for those who want to take that step seriously. It is not a Photoshop course or a printing tutorial: it is complete guidance from the concept to the file ready for the printer.

Three four-hour sessions, separated by intermediate tasks:

- **Session 1** — Concept, format and narrative: what your book is about, how it reads, how many pages, which paper, which printing system.
- **Between sessions** — Physical dummy: scissors, home prints, everything on a table until you have a first clear order.
- **Session 2** — Digital design in Affinity Publisher: from the dummy to the final document.
- **Session 3** — Final review, exports (PDF, ePub, web) and a made-to-measure printing quote.

The workshop is supported by **[112books.eu](https://112books.eu)**, with extensive experience in graphic arts and conceptual communication. During session 3 we work out the printing quote live: size, paper, print run, printing system. Options range from a single copy to short runs for distribution.

The software is **Affinity Publisher**, free since 2024. You need to install it before session 2.

**The only requirement**: having your own photographic body of work — a minimum of 50-100 images from a single project. You do not need to have taken any Llumàtics workshop beforehand.

The workshop runs on demand, four people maximum. The first edition is for students of the *[From Street to Book](/en/tallers/del-carrer-al-llibre/)* course, but any photographer with a project in hand can join the waiting list.
