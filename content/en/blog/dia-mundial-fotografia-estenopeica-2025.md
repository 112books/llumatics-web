On 27 April we are celebrating World Pinhole Photography Day at the Sala Basiana of the Nau Bostik.

The proposal is simple: come, experiment and take photographs with no more structure than each person wants to give it. The AWPCP.org association lends cameras to anyone who does not have one. Some of the results will be developed in the Llumàtics lab.

No plans, no rules and, above all, no lenses.
