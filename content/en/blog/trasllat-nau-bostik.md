After three years in the Poble Sec, Llumàtics is moving to a new location.

In a few weeks we expect to have the new premises ready to resume our usual activity: chemical photography, the real thing, inside the Nau Bostik. There, surrounded by a setting and rooms that are second to none, we will go on offering training, large format sessions and a few surprises we are preparing.
