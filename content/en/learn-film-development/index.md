**Where can I learn to develop film in Barcelona?** At Llumàtics, at Nau Bostik (Carrer Ferran Turné 1-11, La Sagrera, 08027). We run black-and-white film development workshops and C-41 development as B&W, in groups of up to 4 people.

## Where to start

- [Introduction to film development](/en/tallers/iniciacio-revelat/) — the whole process in 3 hours, at Cameras & Films, no experience needed.
- [B&W film development](/en/tallers/revelat-bn/) — 4 hours and two rolls included, to control density, push and pull.
- [Development + printing in one day](/en/tallers/revelat-i-positivat/) — 8 hours for the whole cycle.

## And then

- [Introduction to darkroom printing](/en/tallers/introduccio-al-positivat/) — your first paper prints.
- [Darkroom printing](/en/tallers/copies-en-paper/) — better prints, with fibre paper.
- [C-41 development as B&W](/en/tallers/revelat-color-bn/) and [DIY developers](/en/tallers/reveladors-artesanals/) — to experiment.

## How much it costs

Prices start at €170 (printing) and €220 (B&W development) for one student, dropping to €61–79 per person for a group of four. Materials (rolls and chemicals) are included and training is VAT-exempt.
