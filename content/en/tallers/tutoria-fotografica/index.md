## A different format

Not everyone learns the same way. Some people learn better in a group; others need a space where they don't have to follow anyone else's pace. The tutorial is for the latter — and for the former, when they want to dig into something specific.

It might be to resolve a doubt that's been sitting unanswered for a while. To learn to develop film for the first time without the pressure of a group. To work on a personal photographic project with someone who understands it. To review a series and find out where to improve.

It starts with a brief conversation — by email — to understand where you're coming from and what the session should achieve. The minimum session is **2 hours**; it can be extended to 3 or 4 hours depending on the goal.

## Examples of possible content

- First time in the darkroom: film development step by step
- Review and editing of a personal photographic project
- Learning to make paper prints from your own negatives
- Understanding why your photos don't come out the way you expect
- Exploring a specific technique: large format, pinhole, alternative processes
- Preparation for a commission or an exhibition

## How it works

Write to us and briefly explain what you'd like to work on. We agree on the length and content of the session and propose a date.

Sessions are always individual (1:1) and priced by the hour: **€60/hour, minimum 2 hours**. You can book blocks of 2, 3 or 4 hours.

## What's included

- Individual (1:1) session with Joan Linux
- Use of Llumàtics darkroom and equipment
- Standard darkroom chemicals (if the session includes film development)

## What to bring

- Everything you want to work on: camera, negatives, prints, project, questions
- The session is shaped by a prior conversation — no special preparation needed

## Not included

- Photographic paper — bring what you need; we can advise if necessary
- Film / rolls — Fomapan 35mm and 120 format available at €12/roll
- Photographic papers — pricing on request depending on type, size and finish
