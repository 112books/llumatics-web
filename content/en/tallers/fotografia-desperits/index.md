In the late 19th century, there were photographers who claimed to photograph spirits. In reality they used two things we master: exposure time and double exposure. In this workshop we teach you how they did it and put it into practice for Castanyada.

It will be on **Saturday 31 October, from 7 to 10 pm**, at Nau Bostik.

## What we'll do

1. **The history.** 19th-century spirit photography and the fraud of the spiritualist photographers, with real examples.
2. **The technique.** Long exposures with figures that move or vanish mid-exposure, and double exposures in camera.
3. **The practice.** A session at Nau Bostik in low light, with torches, sheets and costumes to make our own apparitions.
4. **The developing.** You can develop the film with us in a later session, if you like.

## What's included

One black-and-white film per student. If you have a camera with Bulb mode or multiple exposure, bring it; if not, we can lend you one.

## What to bring

**Make-up and characterisation** (make-up, costume and props to create your apparition) are each student's responsibility.

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants to experiment with light and time, with no previous experience needed.

## Sign-up

Price: **60 €**, including one black-and-white film. Places are limited (8). To book your place, [write to us](/en/contacte/?taller=fotografia-desperits#formulari).
