## The street as a first time

There is an enormous difference between photographing in your own spaces —home, family, familiar places— and photographing in the street. On the street you control nothing. The light changes, people move, moments pass in a fraction of a second. And the photographer has to decide: move closer or not, shoot or not, wait or keep walking.

This workshop is for anyone who wants to make that first outing with a method. Not improvised, not left to chance: with tools for looking, for moving, and for understanding what happens when you press the shutter.

Four hours. Theory, walk, development. Small group.

## Key content

- What street photography is (and what it is not)
- The two ways of working: the fisher and the hunter
- Technique for street situations: speed, depth of field, natural light
- The approach: distance, discretion and presence
- Legality in Spain: image rights, public space, limits
- References: a first look at the great authors of the genre

## How the session works

We start at Llumàtics with an hour and a half of theory. It is not a lecture: it is a conversation about how to look, how to move and how to decide. We analyse reference images, discuss concrete cases and answer questions.

Then we go out into the neighbourhood. Camera loaded, brief clear. The teacher goes with the group and is available throughout the walk.

At the end we develop the roll in the lab and do a first collective review of the frames. Not to judge, but to see: what worked, what did not, and why.
