Cookie policy

2 May 2026

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser when you visit it. Cookies allow the website to remember your preferences or actions for a period of time.

Cookies used by this website

This website is built with Hugo (static site generator). It does not use WordPress, databases or tracking scripts. Fonts are self-hosted on our server (no requests to Google Fonts).

Analytics

We use GoatCounter to count visits anonymously. GoatCounter sets no cookies and collects no personal data. goatcounter.com/help/privacy

Contact form

When you submit the contact form, the request is processed by web3forms.com. Web3forms receives your IP address and message content to send the email, but sets no cookies in your browser.

Interactive map

The contact page contains an OpenStreetMap map. The map does not load by default: you must click the “Show map” button to activate it. If you activate it, OpenStreetMap may store technical session cookies in your browser.

First-party cookies

This website generates no first-party cookies.

Conclusion

Under normal use of this website, no cookies are set in your browser without your explicit action. No cookie banner is required because there is no active third-party tracking when the page loads.

Managing cookies

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies at any time:

  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

Updates

We reserve the right to update this policy if the website’s technology stack changes. The revision date appears below the title of this page.
