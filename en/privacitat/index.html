Legal basis

The processing of your data is carried out in accordance with:

  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — EU Regulation 2016/679
  • Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Personal Data Protection and Digital Rights Guarantee (LOPDGDD)

The legal basis for processing is your consent when submitting the contact form or communicating with Llumàtics.

Purposes of processing

The data you provide is used exclusively to:

  1. Respond to your enquiry or workshop request
  2. Manage the training relationship: confirming dates, sending course documentation and materials
  3. Workshop-related communications if you have given explicit consent

No data processing is carried out for commercial or advertising purposes without prior consent.

Data we collect

Through the contact form we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Information you include in your message (workshop of interest, preferred date, etc.)

We do not collect specially protected data (health, ideology, racial origin, etc.).

Recipients

Your data is not shared with third parties, except:

  • When required by law
  • With the form service provider (web3forms.com), which acts as data processor and manages the sending of messages. You can consult their privacy policy at web3forms.com/privacy.

Data retention

Data is retained for the time strictly necessary to handle the enquiry or manage the training relationship. Once the purpose is fulfilled, data is deleted or anonymised, unless the law requires retention for a specific period.

Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection at any time by sending an email to info@llumatics.com with the subject “Data protection” and attaching a copy of your identity document.

If you believe that the processing does not comply with current regulations, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at aepd.es.

Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration. Communication between your browser and this website is encrypted via HTTPS.
