The wonderful world of Hasselblad

Medium format 6×6 with the 500 series. Secrets, tips and plenty of film.

A session devoted entirely to the Hasselblad 500 series: how it works, tips and tricks, double exposures, remote shutter, and a 120 roll you develop the same day.

A different kind of camera

The Hasselblad 500 isn’t a camera you carry everywhere. It’s a camera you use when you want to make a real photograph. The square 6×6cm format, the waist-level finder, the sound of the mirror, the interchangeable film back, the sharpness of Carl Zeiss lenses — everything invites you to slow down, look, and decide.

In this workshop there’s time for everything: to understand the system, to shoot with intention and to see the developed results the same day. We finish with a little something to eat.

Key contents

  • The Hasselblad 500 system: body, dual shutter releases and synchronised operation
  • The interchangeable back: loading a 120 roll and mid-roll swaps
  • The waist-level finder: composition and light reading in square format
  • Lenses: available focal lengths and their uses
  • Double exposures: how to activate them and when to use them
  • Remote shutter and cable: practical possibilities
  • Tips and system quirks that don’t appear in the manuals
  • Practice: a full 120 roll (12 frames 6×6)
  • Roll development and digitisation of all frames

What’s included

  • One 120 B&W roll (12 frames 6×6)
  • Development of the roll on the same day
  • Digitisation of all 12 frames
  • Snack at the end of the session
  • Hasselblad 500 camera available if you don’t own one

What to bring

  • Your own Hasselblad 500 if you have one (not required)

Not included

  • Additional 120 roll (+€12)
  • Paper prints of the frames (Darkroom Printing workshop)

Workshop details

Objective
Master the Hasselblad 500 and its particular features: interchangeable back, dual shutter releases, double exposures and remote shutter.
Methodology
Theory on the camera, then immediate practice with a 120 roll, finishing with development and digitisation of all the frames from the session.
Outcome
A 120 roll (12 frames 6×6) exposed, developed and digitised. Practical, hands-on knowledge of the camera.
Prerequisites
Able to shoot in manual mode. No need to own a Hasselblad — we can lend you one.
Who it's for
Analogue photographers who want to move into medium format, or who already own a Hasselblad and want to get more out of it.
